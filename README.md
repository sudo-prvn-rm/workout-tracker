# 🏋️🧠 45-Day Total Transformation: Muscle & Brain Mastery

A daily progressive workout, calisthenics, cognitive mastery, and PG-friendly budget nutrition system designed to forge an aesthetic, lean physique and an unbreakable, calm, high-IQ mind from **November 17, 2026 to December 31, 2026** (45 days to Jan 1, 2027).

Designed specifically for home training with **2 dumbbells (up to 10kg)** and **calisthenics progressive overload**, paired with **neuroscience-backed brain control protocols** and a **budget Indian PG meal protocol**.

---

## ⚡ Key Features

- **No Database Needed**: Works 100% out of the box in any browser using `localStorage`.
- **Automatic Date Detection & Countdown**: Detects today's date (`new Date()`), counts down the days to Jan 1st, 2027, and anchors to **Today's Mission**.
- **Every Exercise Has a Form Video**: Click `▶ Form Video` on any movement for verified technique breakdowns from top science-based coaches (*Jeff Nippard, Athlean-X, Calisthenicmovement, Squat University, Jeremy Ethier*).
- **Dual Physical + Mental Architecture**:
  - **☀️ Morning Session (60 min)**: Mechanical tension, tempo lifts (3-1-1-0), and calisthenics (Push / Pull / Legs / Upper / Lower).
  - **🧠 Cognitive Mastery & Overthinking Protocol (Daily Brain Mission)**:
    - *How to Stop Overthinking*: The 5-Second Action Rule (Mel Robbins) to bypass hesitation; Zeigarnik Brain Dump; Stoic Dichotomy of Control; Cognitive Defusion.
    - *How to Control the Brain*: Autonomic Nervous System reset with the Physiological Sigh (Huberman/UCLA); 60-min morning phone blackout; Urge Surfing.
    - *How to Be Very Smart*: 90-min single-task Deep Work blocks; Feynman Technique; Mental Models (First Principles, Inversion, 80/20 Pareto, Second-Order Thinking); Active Recall over passive reading.
  - **🌙 Evening Session (30 min)**: Core sculpting (stomach vacuums, hollow body holds, deadbugs), desk posture & hip mobility, and 3,000 brisk steps.
- **Strict PG-Friendly Nutrition & Brain Fuel**:
  - **2 Boiled Eggs Daily** (Monday to Saturday, ₹7/egg = ₹14/day).
  - **Sunday Chicken Special**: PG chicken feast on Sundays (eggs skipped on Sunday as scheduled).
  - **Whey Protein (1 scoop)** + **Creatine Monohydrate (3–5g daily)**: Creatine supports both muscle cell volumization and brain phosphocreatine/ATP recycling for peak mental stamina.
  - **Hydration Tracker**: 4 interactive water badges (3.5L to 4.0L daily).
  - **Cheap Room-Storable Boosters**: Soya chunks (Nutrela), Roasted Bhuna Chana, Roasted Peanuts, Curd/Dahi, and Sattu.
- **Export / Import Backup**: 1-click JSON backup to transfer your progress between your phone and laptop without losing a day.
- **1-Click Reset**: Fresh slate restart from Day 1.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Method 1: Open Directly in Browser (No Server Required!)
Simply double-click `index.html` or drag it into Chrome / Brave / Edge / Firefox / Safari. Everything runs client-side with full persistence.

### Method 2: Local Development Server
```bash
PORT=3001 node server.js
```
Open **[http://localhost:3001](http://localhost:3001)** in your browser.
*(Running locally will also auto-sync completed tasks to `progress.json` on disk).*
