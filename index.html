📖 68-Day Transformation & PG Survival Guide

Everything you need to know to pack on lean muscle and shed fat in a PG room with limited weights.

⚖️ Dumbbell Plate Configuration (Max 10kg)

You have 2 handles, 4 × 2kg plates, and 3kg plates:

  • Light Pair (4kg – 5kg each): Ideal for Strict Lateral Raises, Rear Delt Flyes, and Arnold Press.
  • Medium Pair (7kg – 8kg each): Great for Bicep Curls, Floor Press, and Walking Lunges.
  • Heavy Loaded Single (10kg+): Put 2kg + 3kg on both sides of a single handle. Perfect for Goblet Squats, Bulgarian Split Squats, Single-Arm DB Rows, and Overhead Tricep Extensions!

🧬 The Science of Hypertrophy with 10kg Weights

When you cannot add 20kg of metal, you grow muscle through Mechanical Tension & Muscle Fiber Recruitment:

  • 3-Second Eccentric Tempo: Lower every weight slowly (count 1... 2... 3...). This forces the muscle to withstand tension 3x longer than ordinary gym gym-goers!
  • 1-Second Peak Contraction: Pause at the top of curls and rows to completely eliminate momentum.
  • Unilateral Leverage: Bulgarian split squats and single-arm rows place all load onto ONE muscle. A 10kg single-arm row provides more lats stimulus than a sloppy 25kg barbell row.
  • Progressive Calisthenics: Elevate your feet on your PG bed during pushups to shift 75% of your total bodyweight onto your upper chest and shoulders.

🍳 PG Nutrition & Supplement Protocol

  • Sunday Chicken Special: On Sunday, PG serves chicken dinner! Skip the 2 eggs on Sunday and enjoy the chicken feast.
  • Monday–Saturday Eggs: 2 whole boiled eggs daily (₹7 each = ₹14/day). Eat at breakfast or evening snack.
  • Whey Protein: 1 scoop (24g–25g protein) post-workout or in the evening. Keep your shaker in your room.
  • Creatine Monohydrate: 3g–5g daily. Take it every day at the same time with water or meals. Fully saturates your muscle cells by Week 2 for increased strength and fuller muscles.
  • Water Rule (3.5L – 4.0L): Mandatory with creatine to prevent cramps and maximize muscle cellular volumization.
  • Cheap Protein Boosters: Soya chunks (Nutrela ₹45/box), Roasted Bhuna Chana (₹50/pack), Roasted Peanuts, Curd/Dahi (₹15 packet), and Sattu powder.
